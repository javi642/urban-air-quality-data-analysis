# 🌍 Urban Air Quality Analysis and AQI Prediction

## 📌 Project Overview

Air quality plays a vital role in protecting public health and the environment.  
This project focuses on analyzing **urban air pollution data** to understand **Air Quality Index (AQI) trends**, identify **major contributing pollutants**, and study **seasonal and regional variations** in air quality across different cities in India.

The analysis is performed on a large dataset (~50,000 records) containing pollutant concentrations and meteorological factors. Using Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques, the project provides meaningful insights to support **data-driven environmental decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze air pollution data across multiple regions and time periods  
- Understand factors affecting the Air Quality Index (AQI)  
- Identify major pollutants contributing to poor air quality  
- Study seasonal and temporal AQI trends  
- Visualize pollution patterns using clear and interactive charts  

---

## 📊 Dataset Description
- **Source:** Kaggle (Urban Air Quality Dataset – India)
- **Records:** ~50,000
- **Key Features:**
  - AQI
  - Pollutants: PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃
  - Meteorological factors: Temperature, Humidity, Wind Speed
  - Location details: City, State, Monitoring Station, Coordinates

The dataset enables both **temporal and spatial analysis** of air quality and pollutant behavior.

---

## 🔄 Project Workflow
1. Import and clean air quality dataset (handle missing values & duplicates)
2. Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA)
3. Analyze AQI trends across cities, regions, and seasons
4. Study pollutant variations and their impact on AQI
5. Identify major contributors to air pollution
6. Visualize findings using charts and plots
7. Summarize insights and conclusions

---

## 📈 Key Visualizations
- **AQI Trend Over Time (Line Chart)** – Seasonal variations in air quality
- **Pollutant Contribution (Bar Chart)** – Impact of individual pollutants
- **AQI Comparison Across Cities (Heatmap)** – Regional pollution patterns
- **PM2.5 vs AQI (Scatter Plot)** – Strong positive correlation
- **Pollutant Proportion (Pie Chart)** – Dominant pollutants in urban areas

---

## 🔍 Sample Insights
- AQI levels are generally **higher during winter months**
- **PM2.5** is the major contributor to poor air quality
- Urban and industrial cities show **higher AQI levels**
- AQI tends to be worse on **weekdays** due to traffic and industry
- Rain and wind help reduce pollution, while calm weather worsens it
- Long-term data shows improvement in some cities due to stricter regulations

---

## 📚 Key Learnings
- Seasonal patterns strongly influence air quality
- Fine particulate matter (PM2.5) has the highest health impact
- Human activities play a major role in pollution levels
- Data visualization is essential for identifying pollution trends
- Environmental data analysis supports informed policy decisions
